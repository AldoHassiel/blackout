### evento

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape editorial photograph-style illustration for the next-event card on BLACKOUT, an ethical hacking community website. Create an original fictional cybersecurity conference opening ceremony: a presenter welcoming attendees from a modest stage, viewed from behind seated audience members in a dark modern industrial conference room, a few open laptops visible on desks in the foreground. Natural candid professional event photography, atmospheric overhead spotlights, photographic film grain, subtle halftone texture, believable adults and venue. A wide projection screen behind the presenter contains only a minimal abstract geometric network diagram, absolutely no writing. Landscape 16:9 composition, readable even when displayed as a small 400px-wide card image, clear focal point on presenter and illuminated stage near center. Entirely monochrome black, charcoal and off-white, restrained highlights, deep shadows but discernible scene; matches a minimal dark terminal-inspired website. The scene should communicate anticipation, learning and a real community, not criminal activity. No hoods, masks, weapons, neon, brand logos, Black Hat branding, watermarks, text or typography, no collages or website UI. All people and event are fictional.

Entirely monochrome deep charcoal, black, silvery off-white, subtle analog film grain and restrained print halftone, cinematic practical lighting and gentle monitor glow, bright enough to understand the activity but consistent with a dark minimal terminal-themed website.
